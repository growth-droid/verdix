AC -> district -> Census-2011 district lists for ACs with no usable polygon
===========================================================================
Built 2026-09-29 by build_ac_district_lists.py (re-runnable offline from raw/). Plain public HTTPS GETs only
(curl / python urllib): no login, no cookies we did not receive, no API keys. NO SHRUG data or SHRUG-derived
file is used anywhere here.

OUTPUT FILES (columns: state, ac_no, ac_name, district_current, district_2011, pc_no, pc_name, source_url, note)
  jk_2022_ac_district.csv        90 rows  - J&K after the 2022 delimitation (used for AE 2024, LS 2024)
  assam_2023_ac_district.csv     126 rows - Assam after the 2023 delimitation (used for LS 2024, AE 2026)
  gj_mp_noshape_ac_district.csv  24 rows  - Gujarat 45-56 (Ahmedabad) + 160-167 (Surat), MP 205-208 (Indore);
                                            post-2008 numbering; PCs filled too (from the same 2008 order)
  census2011_district_codes.csv  every district_2011 value -> Census 2011 state + district code (the codes used
                                 by ../hlo and ../pca), so a builder can join on code instead of spelling
  validation.csv                 39 checks, all passing (2 are INFO rows that list app-side reservation-flag errors) (counts, 1..N completeness, reservations, PC coverage,
                                 cross-source agreement). Re-run the script to regenerate.

COLUMN RULES
  ac_name           Spelling exactly as gazetted in the governing delimitation order's Table-A (J&K 2022 order;
                    ECI Assam 2023 order; ECI DPACO 2008), with the "(SC)/(ST)" reservation marker moved to note
                    ("reserved=SC"), en-dashes normalised to "-". Where ECI/CEO operational lists or the app's own
                    ECI-results names spell it differently, the variant is recorded in note - JOIN ON (state, ac_no),
                    not on name. The Assam gazette itself prints several typos (Dibrugrah, Barkhola, Howarghat,
                    Sissibargaon, Ram Krishana Nagar); ECI/CEO 2026 spellings are in note.
  district_current  J&K: the district heading in the 2022 order (still the 20 districts today).
                    Assam: the district in CEO Assam's 23-03-2026 final roll list (35 districts) with CEO short labels
                    expanded (Kamrup Metro -> Kamrup Metropolitan, Sibsagar -> Sivasagar, South Salmara ->
                    South Salmara-Mankachar). The 2023 order used 31 districts (Bajali, Tamulpur, Hojai, Biswanath had
                    been merged back into their parents on 31-12-2022 and were re-created on 25-08-2023, each
                    defined as a set of the NEW ACs); whenever the 2023 heading differs, note says so.
                    GJ/MP: district heading in the 2008 order (unchanged since).
  district_2011     The Census 2011 district (Census spelling, e.g. Baramula, Badgam, Bandipore, Shupiyan, Punch,
                    Ahmadabad) that the current district is, or was carved from:
                      Bajali<-Barpeta, Tamulpur<-Baksa, Hojai<-Nagaon, Biswanath<-Sonitpur, Charaideo<-Sivasagar,
                      Majuli<-Jorhat, West Karbi Anglong<-Karbi Anglong, South Salmara-Mankachar<-Dhubri,
                      Sribhumi = Karimganj renamed (Nov-2024).
                    No district in these lists was carved from TWO 2011 parents, so no row has a split parent.
  pc_no/pc_name     From the same order's Table-B. J&K PCs 1-5 (18 ACs each); Assam PCs 1-14 in Table-B order
                    (matches the app's 2024 GE numbering). Reservation of PCs: Assam 1 Kokrajhar ST, 6 Diphu ST,
                    8 Silchar SC; Gujarat 8 Ahmedabad West SC; J&K none.

CAVEATS (honest limits)
  * district_2011 is by DISTRICT LINEAGE, not a spatial or village-level join (these ACs have no polygon). It is
    exact for J&K (no district created since 2007; the order's territorial units are as on 15-06-2020), for the
    GJ/MP city seats (wholly inside Ahmedabad City taluka / Surat City taluka / Indore tehsil municipal wards), and
    for Assam districts whose outer boundary is unchanged. In Assam's Bodoland (BTR) belt, 2011 revenue circles
    straddled districts (e.g. Census 2011 lists Goreswar, Rangia, Tihu, Bajali, Sarupeta circles as "(Pt)" in both
    Baksa and Kamrup/Nalbari/Barpeta). If any village moved between districts between 2011 and the 01-01-2023
    freeze, an AC near those borders could hold a sliver of a neighbouring 2011 district; a village-level crosswalk
    (option 2) is needed to quantify that.
  * The Assam order is a scanned PDF. It was OCR'd (RapidOCR, raw/assam_final_order_ocr_p51-82.json) and every
    name that disagrees with ECI/CEO spellings was checked by eye against the page image; the OCR reproduces the
    gazette's own spellings. AC numbers, 126/126 completeness, 9 SC / 19 ST, 31 districts and 14 PCs (1 SC / 2 ST)
    all validate against the order's own stated totals.
  * Found in passing (app data, not changed here): the app's public/data/seats_ae.json reservation flag `r`
    disagrees with the orders for 5 J&K-2024 ACs (55, 61, 68, 75, 78 flagged SC; all GEN) and 21 Assam-2026 ACs
    (e.g. 1 Gossaigaon SC, 30 Hajo-Sualkuchi ST instead of SC), and seats_ge.json flags Assam-2024 PCs 1 Kokrajhar SC,
    3 Barpeta ST, 5 Guwahati ST (official: 1 Kokrajhar ST, 6 Diphu ST, 8 Silchar SC). It looks like the
    PRE-delimitation reservation carried over by seat number (confirmed for the Assam PCs: the app's own 2009/2014
    rows have PC1 Karimganj SC, PC3 Autonomous District ST, PC5 Kokrajhar ST; not traced for the ACs). The order's reservation is in note ("reserved=").
  * J&K CEO 2024 roll prints "CHAH-I-SHARIEF" (AC 30) and "BHADERWAH" (AC 51); the order prints Chrar-i-Sharief and
    Bhadarwah. The order's Table-A prints "Thannamandi" (AC 87) but its Table-B prints "Thanamandi". All recorded.
